About

Thayam Game is a casual online board game based on the traditional Tamil dice game Thayam. Play with 2 to 4 players against friends, using a private room and invite link, or against the computer.

Get it on Google Play

Is it free?

Yes. Thayam Game is free to download and play. It is supported by ads. Players can make an optional one-time purchase to remove ads.
