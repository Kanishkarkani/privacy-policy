1. Introduction

Welcome to Thayam Game. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

2. About the App

Thayam Game is a classic board game where players can:

  • Select the number of players before starting
  • Play with friends by creating a room and sharing an invite link
  • Play against the computer (AI opponent)

3. Information We Collect

a) Information You Provide

We do not require you to create an account or provide any personal information (such as your name or email) to use this app.

b) Automatically Collected Information (via Third-Party SDKs)

We use third-party SDKs for ads and analytics, which may automatically collect the following:

  • Device information – device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage data – how you interact with the app, session duration, game events
  • IP address – used to determine approximate location (country/region level)
  • Advertising ID – used to serve relevant ads

5. Multiplayer & Room Sharing

When you use the Create Room feature to play with friends:

  • A room code or link is generated for session matching
  • No personal data (name, email, phone number) is collected or stored
  • Room sessions are temporary and not permanently logged

6. How We Use Information

Data collected through third-party SDKs is used to:

  • Display advertisements within the app
  • Understand how players use the app to improve gameplay
  • Monitor app stability and fix crashes

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

7. Data Retention

We do not store any personal data on our own servers. Data collected by third-party SDKs is retained according to their respective policies.

8. Children's Privacy

Thayam Game is a family-friendly game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.
